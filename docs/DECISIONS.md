# Decisions

Why Clara 1.0 is built the way it is. The plan ([clara-rebuild-plan.md](../clara-rebuild-plan.md)) set the direction;
the questions below were answered by the owner before the rewrite (October 2026); the last section lists the choices
made while building, to be confirmed or changed.

## Answered by the owner

| Question | Decision |

## Automations: one kind, regular expressions, a tree of triggers (owner's decision, November 2026)

A schedule (at a time) and a hook (when something happens) are one **automation**, with one list, one API, one command, one set of
tools. Its filters are regular expressions; its trigger is a free tree of AND and OR over events, instants and conditions of time. It
**supersedes** the rows marked « superseded 2026-11-13 » above (the schedules' and hooks' vocabulary, `deliver`, and the row « No
regular expression in a filter »), whose behaviour is kept wherever this entry does not say otherwise. The web page and form are

## Dictation drops the segments Whisper itself calls silence (2026-11-23)

A real test sent about 25 seconds of near silence and a little room noise and got the text « Merci. », a known Whisper hallucination;
the client put it in the chat box instead of saying « Nothing heard ». The audio was not kept, so that case could not be replayed.

| Decision | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The worker drops a segment whose `no_speech_prob` is above 0.6, on that alone.** A dictation where every segment is dropped returns `text: ""`, which the API already treats as silence (nothing made, tainted or sent). No list of banned phrases, no setting. | faster-whisper only drops a segment when `no_speech_prob > 0.6` **and** `avg_logprob < -1.0`. Measured on the real `small` int8 model with the language forced to `fr` and to `en`, the hallucinations on digital silence, white and pink noise from -70 to -30 dBFS, clicks and breaths ("Sous-titres réalisés par la communauté d'Amara.org", "You", "Sous-titrage ST' 501") had `no_speech_prob` 0.64 to 0.89 and `avg_logprob` -0.39 to -1.0: the second condition never held, so the library never dropped them. Synthetic French speech (the three samples, 3 to 30 s) stays at 0.03 to 0.19; the same speech made very quiet (-55 dBFS rms) or with noise at 0 dB signal-to-noise reaches 0.50 at most in its first segments and 0.55 in a closing « Merci beaucoup pour ton aide » (222 segments kept, none dropped). The threshold sits between 0.55 and 0.64: a margin of 0.04 above the quietest speech tried, 0.4 above the speech samples. `no_speech_prob` is per 30-second window, so every segment of a window shares it. | `dictation/worker.py::is_speech`, `NO_SPEECH_MAX` |
| **What was not reproduced.** With `vad_filter=True` (what the worker uses) none of the synthetic noise produced any segment at all: the voice activity detector removed it. The hallucinations above appear only with the detector off, so the owner's « Merci. » got past the detector on real room noise, and its `no_speech_prob` was not recorded. The filter is proven on what the decoder says about noise, not on the owner's recording. | If « Merci. » had a `no_speech_prob` under 0.6 it still gets through; lowering the threshold would then start to cost quiet real speech (0.55 measured). That trade is the owner's, with a real recording. | `tests/test_dictation.py` |
